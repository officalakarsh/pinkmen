## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a synthesis of historic Rajasthani royal patronage and razor-sharp contemporary high luxury. Rooted in the storied gemstone ateliers of Jaipur, the aesthetic marries traditional craftsmanship—kundan, meenakari, uncut polki diamonds, and heritage filigree—with the restrained, deliberate whitespace of modern editorial design.

The target audience comprises discerning global and domestic collectors of fine bespoke jewellery, high-net-worth grooms, and connoisseurs seeking heirloom investments. The visual experience must evoke reverence, regal warmth, tactile opulence, and modern effortless ease. Every interaction should feel akin to an exclusive private viewing in a Jaipur haveli palace salon: unhurried, exquisitely detailed, and impeccably curated.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from the royal courts of Rajasthan, balancing rich historic pigments with soft, luminous materials:

- **Primary (`#58111A` / Heritage Royal Maroon):** Used for primary buttons, prominent editorial headlines, active navigation states, and high-impact luxury accents. A deep variant (`#6B1724`) serves hover and active states.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` / Polished Royal Gold):** Accompanied by a brighter champagne gold (`#D4AF37`), used for delicate borders, authentic hallmark accents, filigree dividers, rating stars, and subtle iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#1B4332` / Jewel Emerald):** An authentic nod to royal emerald carving; reserved for sustainability labels, natural certification badges, WhatsApp brand touchpoints, and bespoke prestige status tags.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Base Canvas (`#FAF8F5`): Warm raw silk ivory.
  - Surface Subdued (`#F5F0EB`): Secondary container and product card display background.
  - Surface Accent (`#F3E8E2`): Delicate blush / rose gold wash for badge fills and gentle notification banners.
  - Text Primary (`#231B1B`): Deep obsidian charcoal, preventing harsh pure-black contrast against cream.
  - Text Secondary (`#6E6259`): Aged parchment grey for secondary product specs, dimensions, and metadata.

## Typography

The type system balances evocative editorial romanticism with crisp transactional utility:

- **Headings (Playfair Display):** High-contrast transitional serifs provide regal stateliness and editorial gravitas. Use generous vertical rhythm and subtle letter tracking. Large display sizes evoke bespoke luxury catalogue spreads.
- **Body & UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A modern geometric sans with warm, open counters. Delivers effortless legibility across dense gemstone specifications, carat weight charts, and certification descriptions.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with elevated letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) and uppercase styling for authenticity markers, hallmarks (e.g., "BIS 916 HALLMARKED"), and category slugs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces high-end editorial composition with disciplined structure:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Maximum canvas width of `1440px`. Column gutters set to `1.5rem` (`24px`), with outer canvas margins expanding dynamically from `4rem` (`64px`) to `6rem` (`96px`) on ultra-wide screens to retain intimate containment.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Breakpoint between `768px` and `1024px`, utilizing `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `2.5rem` (`40px`) outer margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Fluid grid under `768px` with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins, ensuring full edge-to-edge breathing room without horizontal clipping.
- **Rhythm:** Generous vertical sections separated by deliberate negative space (`space-xl` through double extra-large section paddings of `5rem`), treating jewellery as art pieces rather than commodities.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids aggressive, deep dropshadows in favor of ethereal, ambient illumination:

- **Ambient Light Shadows:** Soft, multi-layered golden-amber tints emulate warm palace lighting:
  - Low Elevation (Product Cards): `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(88, 17, 26, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.06)`.
  - Medium Elevation (Sticky Header, Floating Concierge): `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(88, 17, 26, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`.
  - High Elevation (Cart Drawer, Quick-view Modals): `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(35, 27, 27, 0.15)`.
- **Low-Contrast Filament Borders:** Surfaces are paired with hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`) to delineate edges with jeweller's precision.
- **Frosted Silk Glassmorphism:** Sticky navigation and floating bars utilize a frosted background of `rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.88)` with a `16px` backdrop-blur filter to let rich gemstone imagery glide underneath effortlessly.

## Shapes

The primary architectural shape language is **Sharp (`0`)**, referencing cut precious stones, classic museum display vitrines, and bespoke architectural archways.

- **Foundational Containers & Images:** Product imagery frames, structural cards, dropdown menus, and primary button containers use strict `0px` border radius, asserting unyielding classic formality.
- **Contrasting Pill Accents:** Pill shapes (`9999px`) are intentionally reserved as an accent language strictly for micro-elements: authenticity badges (e.g., "Jaipur Karigari", "22K Gold"), product filter tags, and the floating WhatsApp concierge toggle. This deliberate tension between architectural rectilinearity and soft pill seals mirrors traditional royal wax seals placed on formal parchment.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Heirloom Maroon):** Solid `#58111A` background, ivory text (`#FAF8F5`), `0px` radius, uppercase `label-md` tracking, accompanied by a subtle `1px` inner border inset. Hover transition softly deepens to `#6B1724` with a golden micro-underline sweep.
- **Secondary Action (Champagne Outline):** Hairline `1px` border in `#C5A059`, transparent background, maroon text (`#58111A`). On hover, fills with `#F5F0EB` tint.
- **Concierge Action (Direct WhatsApp):** Pill-shaped button featuring `#1B4332` (Jewel Emerald) or `#25D366` accent with silk ivory typography, accompanied by a subtle pulsating gold ring.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean vertical card enclosed in an optional hairline border (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)`).
- **Image Frame:** `4:5` vertical aspect ratio against `#F5F0EB` background, allowing white and yellow gold to stand out crisp without clipping shadows.
- **Overlays:** Top-left corner houses pill-shaped artisan seals ("Handcrafted in Jaipur"); top-right features an ultra-delicate wishlist heart icon in gold hairline stroke.
- **Content:** Product title in `headline-sm`, purity/material subtitle in `body-sm` (`#6E6259`), followed by price formatted in `body-md` bold with the Indian Rupee (₹) symbol.

### Chips & Badges
- **Hallmark & Authenticity Badges:** Pill-shaped capsules with `#FAF8F5` background, `1px solid #C5A059`, featuring uppercase `label-sm` text. An emerald dot indicates BIS hallmark verification.
- **Filter Tags:** Rectangular or softly pill-formed, utilizing `#F3E8E2` background and maroon text when selected.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Bottom-border-only or understated `1px` hairline box with `#FAF8F5` fill. The border shifts to polished gold (`#C5A059`) upon focus, with a floating label shifting to `label-sm`.
- **Ring & Bangle Sizing Selector:** Grid of square pill boxes with clear millimeter and Indian/UK standard markings; active selection marked by maroon fill and gold stroke.

### Header & Sticky Concierge
- **Sticky Navigation:** Translucent ivory silk background (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.9)`), centered regal wordmark, split left-navigation (Collections, Bespoke, Heritage), and right utilities (Currency Switcher, Search bar, Wishlist count, Bag count with a subtle maroon badge).
- **Floating WhatsApp Concierge:** Fixed to the bottom-right corner (`24px` offset on mobile, `32px` on desktop). Includes an avatar chip showing a royal stylist status and the prompt: "Consult Jaipur Stylist".
